# Sound sources

## Goal cheer

`public/audio/goal-cheer.mp3` is the unmodified MP3 **Shouts and Applauses of Teens #1**, sound 236, by **DenisChardonnet**, hosted by BigSoundBank. The source describes a studio group saying “Yeah” and applauding; it is a recorded crowd reaction, not an actual football stadium recording.

- [Original sound and license declaration](https://bigsoundbank.com/shouts-and-applauses-of-teens-1-s0236.html)
- [Original MP3](https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/0236.mp3)

- [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The source explicitly permits downloading, modifying, redistributing and commercial use without payment, account creation or mandatory attribution. Source and license were checked on 8 September 2026. We retain this credit for provenance even though attribution is optional.

Original file: 172,432 bytes. SHA-256: `fb6b711aaaf738e9646cb9b210c40a152cba8a53b38e24b2a15f4b0201ffda20`.

Playback uses the original pitch at reduced gain, only after a recorded goal. The file is served locally with the site. It is fetched and decoded once after the first audio gesture; if loading has not completed or fails, the goal whistle remains and the cheer is skipped. An arriving download never starts a past cheer.

## Original synthesis

Drums, ball contacts, post sound and referee whistles are generated by this project's `src/audio/stadium-sounds.ts`. No outside recording, model call or external audio service is used for those sounds. The rejected continuous noise bed, harmonic synthetic crowd voices and procedural claps/applause are absent. The recorded goal reaction naturally includes applause; it is separate from the removed synthetic backbeat/save sounds.

The user approved the whistle while listening to the development preview. Automated checks cover sample bounds, timing and audio lifecycle; no agent audition of the recorded cheer is claimed.
